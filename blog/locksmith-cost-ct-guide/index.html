The Bait and Switch Scam: What Actually Happens in Fairfield County CT

You search "locksmith near me," click one of the top Google results in Norwalk or Stamford, and you call. The person who answers is a call center, not a local locksmith. They quote you $35 and say the tech is 20 minutes away. The tech arrives in an unmarked car with no company ID and a generic receipt pad. He opens your car in three minutes, then hands you a bill for $350. The original $35 was the "service call." Labor, tools, mileage are all extra. By now it is 11 PM, the car is already open. You pay.

Reddit threads are full of people paying $475 to $600 for a simple car lockout. In Fairfield County CT, a legitimate lockout runs $100 to $200 during the day. Not $475. If anyone refuses to quote you a firm price before dispatch, that refusal is the warning.

The red flags are always the same:

  • Refuses to give a price range over the phone
  • Tech arrives in an unmarked vehicle with no company ID
  • Receipt has no business name, phone number, or license number
  • Demands cash only, Zelle, or Venmo
  • Price is dramatically different from the phone quote once on site

Lock Rekey vs. Lock Replacement in CT

Rekeying is the right move in most situations where the lock itself is in good shape. When you rekey a cylinder, the locksmith changes the internal pins so the old key no longer works and cuts you new keys. At $35 to $60 per cylinder, it is the most cost effective way to regain control of who has access to your home.

The situations where rekeying makes sense: you just moved into a new place in Fairfield or Norwalk and have no idea how many copies of the old key are out there; you ended a relationship or fired someone who had a key; you lost a key and cannot account for it.

Lock replacement makes sense when the hardware itself is failing, worn, or a lower security grade than you want. Many homes across Fairfield County still have builder-grade knob locks that offer minimal protection. Upgrading to a Grade 1 deadbolt changes that. A new deadbolt installation in Fairfield County runs $170 to $340, which includes both the hardware and the labor. High security options from Medeco or Mul-T-Lock run toward the upper end of that range. See our full price list for a breakdown by lock brand and grade.

What Moves the Locksmith Cost CT Price Up or Down

Two jobs that sound identical on the phone can land at different prices for legitimate reasons. Here is what actually affects cost:

  • Time of day. After hours calls carry a 30 to 40 percent premium over standard daytime rates. If you need a locksmith at 2 AM in Westport, expect to pay toward the top of the after hours range. We tell you this upfront, not after we arrive.
  • Vehicle or lock complexity. Standard Honda and Toyota vehicles are straightforward. Luxury European models, electric vehicles, and vehicles with proximity key systems require specialized equipment. The price reflects actual job time and tooling cost, not an arbitrary markup.
  • Number of cylinders rekeyed. Rekeying is priced per cylinder. A front door with both a deadbolt and a knob lock is two cylinders. Most homes in Fairfield County run two to four cylinders total depending on how many entry points have locks.
  • Whether you have a working key. For car key replacement, having one working original key allows the locksmith to clone it directly, which can shave cost and time. With no working key at all, the locksmith generates the cut data from your VIN, which takes a little longer.
  • Lock hardware grade. Replacing a builder-grade lock with a high security Schlage B60N or Medeco deadbolt costs more because the hardware itself costs more. Labor is consistent; hardware grade drives the upper range.
