The Scam Mechanics: How It Works in Westchester

The bait and switch locksmith scam is well-documented, and Westchester residents are a common target. A company buys Google ads using a local 914 phone number, accumulates hundreds of purchased five-star reviews, and positions itself above real businesses. You call, a dispatch center quotes you $19 or $35, and a contractor shows up in an unmarked car. The price triples once the job is done or your keys are in hand. The tech pushes for Zelle or cash and tells you the card reader is broken.

One Reddit commenter described being alone at 11 PM with her dog in the locked car. She panicked and went with whoever picked up fastest. That is the emotional state the scam targets every time.

The tell is simple: any real locksmith in Hartsdale or Greenburgh gives you a firm price range over the phone before anyone moves. If they refuse, hang up.

Deadbolt Replacement and Rekeying After a Move in Greenburgh

One of the most overlooked locksmith needs in the Greenburgh area involves new residents who move into a house or apartment and never change the locks. The previous tenant or owner may have given out keys to housekeepers, contractors, dog walkers, and family members over the years. You have no way of knowing how many copies of your key exist.

Rekeying is the fastest and cheapest solution. A locksmith reconfigures the lock cylinder so that your old key no longer works and a new key is cut. For most standard deadbolts and knob locks, rekeying costs $35 to $60 per cylinder. For a front door with a deadbolt and a knob lock, that is typically $70 to $120 total. A full lock replacement with new hardware runs $170 to $340 per door depending on the brand and security level of the lock chosen.

High security locks, such as those from Medeco or Mul-T-Lock, run toward the top of that range but offer patented key control, meaning keys cannot be duplicated at a hardware store without authorization. For Greenburgh residents in densely accessed buildings, co op units, or homes where prior owners had long tenancies with many distributed keys, high security hardware is worth the additional cost.
