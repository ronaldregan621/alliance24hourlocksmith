What to Do While You Wait for an Emergency Locksmith in Fairfield County

Once you have an ETA, these steps make the rest of the call go faster.

  1. Move somewhere safe and lit. If you are on the shoulder of the Merritt Parkway or Route 7, get as far from moving traffic as possible. If it is late, find a gas station or store entrance. Do not wait alone in an unlit area.
  2. Have your ID and proof of ownership ready. For a home lockout: photo ID plus a utility bill, lease, or piece of mail. For a car lockout: registration or title accessible on your phone if the originals are locked inside.
  3. Give us a precise location. A street address with town is better than an intersection. If you are in a parking structure, the level number and nearest space number help us find you in a building where cell signal may be weak.
  4. Do not attempt to force entry. Coat hanger attempts and window wedging cause paint scratches and sometimes window cracks. A professional unlock takes 5 to 15 minutes and costs far less than body shop repairs.
